Outreach

Robotics should leave the pit.

The strongest programs do more than compete. They make engineering visible to younger students, families, schools, and the community that supports them.

Our approach
01

Teach, don't just demonstrate.

Whenever possible, outreach should give participants something to try: build, program, test, or solve.

02

Document what students learn.

Photos, write-ups, build notes, and demonstrations turn one event into a resource other teams and students can use.

03

Build relationships.

Schools, local organizations, sponsors, mentors, and other robotics teams make the ecosystem stronger.
