What we do
01

Engineering

CAD, fabrication, mechanisms, electronics, software, testing, and the decisions that connect them.

02

Competition

Matches turn prototypes into evidence. We use results, failures, and repeatability to decide what changes next.

03

Community

Outreach, documentation, sponsorship, and mentoring keep the program connected to the people around it.

The principle

Students should own the work.

Mentors and supporters make the program stronger, but the goal is not to build a robot for students. It is to give students the tools, time, and guidance to build it themselves.
